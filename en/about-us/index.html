| WHO WE ARE

Experience
that translates into quality.

KROFFT specialises in the complete production of made-to-measure furniture, fitted interiors and furnishings. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology, precision and attention to every detail.

We create interiors for apartments and residences, commercial spaces, clinics and medical facilities, service venues, public buildings and the marine industry. We deliver bespoke projects and large contract developments.

Our goal is to deliver durable, attractive and functional solutions tailored to each client. We ensure consistency at every stage, from documentation and production preparation to manufacturing and final installation.
